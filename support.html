Common questions

Where are my PDFs? In the app, and in the Files app under On My iPhone, Smart PDF Converter.

Does it upload my files? No. Everything runs on your iPhone, and the app works in Airplane Mode.

I forgot a PDF's password. It cannot be recovered. The app does not keep passwords.

Can it turn a PDF back into Word, or read text from a scan? Not yet. It does not convert PDFs to Word and does not do OCR.

A Word, Excel or PowerPoint file looks different. Complex layouts are simplified. Email us an example you are able to share, and we will look at it.
